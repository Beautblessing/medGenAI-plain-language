MASTER PROMPT 5.2 — SUBMISSION SCOPE: PROFESSIONAL NARRATIVE vs PLAIN-LANGUAGE OUTPUT vs REFERENCE PATIENT EXPLANATION
Prompt version: 5.2.1 (derived from Master Prompt 5.1.0; 5.2.0 removed anonymization, 5.2.1 also removes summarization)
Application: medGenAI_4_1
Submission priority: functional, reproducible research prototype for 10 November 2026

WHAT CHANGES FROM 5.1
Everything in 5.1 (and, through it, 5.0) still applies except the scope changes below.

1. SCOPE OF THE SUBMITTED EVALUATION
  In scope: one focused, three-way comparison per patient (HC0001–HC0010) and language (EN/ES):
    (a) the professional clinical narrative (source, from the approved v9 freeze),
    (b) the generated plain-language output (local model Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and the rule baseline),
    (c) the reference patient explanation (from the approved v9 freeze), scored as the plain-language baseline.
  Compared on: length and sentence length, readability (primary score, Flesch-Kincaid grade for English),
  required structured facts supported, critical problems, contradictions / unsupported additions (NLI),
  semantic closeness to the professional narrative (embedding cosine), and similarity of the output to the
  reference explanation (BERTScore F1 / embedding cosine). Also English vs Spanish and method vs method.
  Out of scope: anonymization and summarization.

2. WHY THESE TWO TASKS ARE OUT OF SCOPE
  Anonymization: the ten cases are synthetic and contain only known fictional identifiers (SYN-ID, SYN-PHONE,
  example.invalid addresses). Replacing them is deterministic and its outcome is known in advance, so it is a
  preprocessing step, not an NLP result. Its NLI checks also report false contradictions on unchanged sentences.
  Summarization: the approved freeze has no reference summaries, so a summary can only be checked against the
  source, not against an approved target. The reference patient explanations are an approved target for the
  plain-language task, which makes that comparison the stronger and more defensible result. One task also keeps
  the results, the review effort and the write-up focused.

3. WHAT THIS MEANS IN THE APPLICATION
  - The Anonymization and Summarization tabs are hidden. Batch generation and CSV batch offer only
    plain-language adaptation; a CSV row with a hidden task is rejected with a clear error.
  - Plain-Language Adaptation and Evaluation and Metrics show a three-way table first: Measure | Professional
    narrative (source) | Generated plain-language output | Reference patient explanation. The reference column is
    computed deterministically on the fly and is not stored as a run.
  - Hidden-task runs, review items and output files are excluded from the Evaluation and Metrics run list, the
    Evaluation Review Queue and the export bundle; the export manifest records scope_note and
    excluded_tasks = ["anonymization", "summarization"].
  - The code and tests for both tasks are retained. Either can be shown again for inspection with
    CLINICAL_EVAL_ENABLE_ANONYMIZATION=1 and/or CLINICAL_EVAL_ENABLE_SUMMARIZATION=1 (exports then include it).
  - No data are deleted: earlier runs stay in runtime_eval/ and are simply not shown or exported.

4. ACCEPTANCE (adjusted)
  The 5.0/5.1 transformation gate applies to plain-language adaptation only. All other gates, Workspace A
  preservation (data, approvals and hashes unchanged) and isolation tests remain as in 5.1.
