PLAIN-LANGUAGE ADAPTATION PROMPT (plain_v7_sectionwise_strict) — Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, greedy decoding

How it works: identifiers are removed from the professional narrative; the narrative is split into sections by its
headings (follow-up sentences with urgent wording become warning signs); each section is sent to the model on its own
with the messages below; the outputs are joined under the fixed headings. No example patient is included.
